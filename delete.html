Account and data deletion

Delete your SphereMessage account

Use this page to request permanent deletion of your SphereMessage account and the data associated with it. You do not need to reinstall or open the app to submit a request.

🗑️

Send a deletion request

Enter enough information for us to locate and verify your account. Never include your password or one-time verification code.

No email app opened?

Send the prepared request manually to:

spheremessage@gmail.com 

                    

                


                

                    

                        
What happens next

                        
    
                            
  1. 
                                01
                                
    Identity check
    We may contact you using information already connected to the account to confirm that the request is genuine.
    
                            
    2. 
                            
  2. 
                                02
                                
    Account deletion
    After verification, the account is permanently deleted rather than temporarily disabled or frozen.
    
                            
    3. 
                            
  3. 
                                03
                                
    Confirmation
    We send confirmation when the request has been completed. We aim to process verified requests promptly and generally within 30 days.

Limited retention

                        

                            Limited information may be retained only where reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal obligations or dispute resolution. Residual copies may also remain temporarily in routine backups until they are overwritten. Retained information is restricted from normal product use.
                        

                    

                

            


            

                
You can also request deletion inside the app

                
Open SphereMessage and go to Profile → Settings → Account → Delete account. The web request above remains available if you have already uninstalled the app or cannot access your account.
