Account and data deletion

Delete your SphereMessage account

Use this page to permanently delete your SphereMessage account and the data associated with it. You do not need to reinstall or open the app to submit a request.

🗑️

Verify and delete your account

Enter your account email. We’ll send a one-time code so you can verify ownership before confirming deletion.

What happens next

  1. 01

    Identity check

    Enter the six-digit code sent to the email connected to your account.

  2. 02

    Account deletion

    After verifying your email, confirm that you want to permanently delete your account.

  3. 03

    Confirmation

    This page confirms when your account deletion is complete.

Data covered by the request

  • • Account identifiers, profile information and SM Number
  • • Photos, bio, preferences, hobbies and love-language information
  • • Location-related profile data and visibility settings
  • • Messages, interests, matches, posts, comments and likes
  • • Moments, Hangout participation and other in-app activity

Limited retention

Limited information may be retained only where reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal obligations or dispute resolution. Residual copies may also remain temporarily in routine backups until they are overwritten. Retained information is restricted from normal product use.
